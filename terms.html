Terms and Conditions

These Terms explain the basic rules for using MindCare by WarmHand Impact Group while the platform is in MVP/private testing and preparing for launch.

Service purpose

MindCare by WarmHand Impact Group is designed to help users connect with verified social workers online. The platform supports access, scheduling, communication, and service management.

User responsibilities

  • Use accurate account information.
  • Treat other users, workers, and support staff respectfully.
  • Do not harass, threaten, impersonate, exploit, or abuse any person through the platform.
  • Do not use the platform for emergencies or immediate danger situations.
  • Follow payment, booking, cancellation, and attendance rules shown inside the app.

Worker responsibilities

Workers must provide honest profile information, follow professional boundaries, attend accepted sessions, protect user privacy, and follow platform rules shown during onboarding and service use.

Payments and payouts

Clients must review the displayed session price before confirming payment. Workers must review the displayed payout and attendance rules before accepting paid work. Payment and payout details may differ by market and service type and should be checked inside the app before confirmation.

AI boundaries

AI supports matching, system checking, and product development under human oversight. AI does not replace social workers and does not provide therapy, diagnosis, clinical decisions, or emergency care.

Availability

Because the platform is in MVP/private testing and preparing for launch, some services, regions, features, or worker availability may be limited.

Account actions

MindCare by WarmHand Impact Group may restrict or close accounts that breach safety, privacy, payment, or conduct rules. Users may request account deletion through the account deletion page.

Contact

For terms questions, contact mindcarezw@gmail.com.

Emergency note: MindCare is not an emergency service. If someone is in immediate danger, they should contact local emergency services or a trusted nearby person immediately.
