03 / Project in development

MindCare: connecting clients and social workers

MVP · private testing · not publicly launched

For clients

MindCare is intended to make it easier for people seeking psychosocial support to connect with suitable, verified social workers for structured, human-led conversations.

For social workers

The platform is designed to provide organised service access and workflows for matching, scheduling and communication, with appropriate safeguards and professional boundaries.

How it is being built

Requirements and user journeys are translated into iterative Flutter and Supabase development, issue tracking, staged testing, documented evidence and review of safety and privacy constraints. Features are not described as operational until verified.

Watch MindCare demonstration video

MindCare is under development. The video is a demonstration, not evidence of a public launch or measured user outcomes. MindCare is not an emergency service or a replacement for clinical or crisis care.
