Safety boundary

MindCare is not an emergency service. If someone is in immediate danger, they should contact local emergency services or a trusted nearby person immediately.

AI boundary

AI supports matching, system checking, and product development under human oversight. AI does not replace social workers and does not provide therapy, diagnosis, clinical decisions, or emergency care.
