WarmHand by MindCare helps people find human support online.

WarmHand by MindCare is a developing digital support platform designed to help people connect with verified social workers online. The product is being prepared carefully before wider public launch.

Contact support Read privacy policy

Important safety note

MindCare is not an emergency service. If someone is in immediate danger, they should contact local emergency services or a trusted nearby person immediately.

AI supports matching, system checking, and product development under human oversight. AI does not replace social workers and does not provide therapy, diagnosis, clinical decisions, or emergency care.

Human-first support

WarmHand by MindCare is built around access to verified social workers, clear service boundaries, and respectful support.

Careful product stage

The platform is in MVP/private testing and preparing for launch. Public claims will remain honest and evidence-based.

Legal and support pages

This website provides public access to privacy, terms, account deletion, and support information for users and reviewers.

About the project

WarmHand by MindCare is being developed under the broader WarmHand Impact Group direction. The mission is to make access to social-work-informed support more organized, transparent, and reachable through digital tools.
