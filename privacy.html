Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how MindCare by WarmHand Impact Group handles information while the platform is in MVP/private testing and preparing for launch.

Information we may collect

  • Account details such as name, email address, role, and login information.
  • Profile details needed to match users with suitable support options.
  • Session, booking, support, and safety-related records needed to operate the service.
  • Technical information such as device, app version, and diagnostic logs used to improve reliability.

How information is used

Information is used to operate accounts, support matching, manage bookings, provide support, protect users, improve the product, and meet lawful administrative requirements.

AI use

AI supports matching, system checking, and product development under human oversight. AI does not replace social workers and does not provide therapy, diagnosis, clinical decisions, or emergency care.

Sharing

Information is shared only where needed to operate the service, support users, meet lawful duties, or work with trusted service providers who help run the platform.

Retention

Records may be kept for as long as needed for account support, safety, dispute handling, service improvement, legal compliance, and operational accountability. Deletion requests are reviewed against these needs.

Your choices

Users may request access, correction, or deletion of their account information by using the account deletion page or contacting support.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact mindcarezw@gmail.com.

Emergency note: MindCare is not an emergency service. If someone is in immediate danger, they should contact local emergency services or a trusted nearby person immediately.
