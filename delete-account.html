Request account deletion

Users can request deletion of their WarmHand by MindCare account using the steps below.

How to request deletion

  1. Email mindcarezw@gmail.com from the email address connected to your account.
  2. Use the subject line: Account deletion request.
  3. Include your full name, account email, and role if known, such as client, worker, or admin user.
  4. Support will review the request and confirm the next step.

What may be deleted

Account profile information and non-required account records may be deleted or deactivated after identity and ownership checks.

What may be retained

Some records may need to be retained for safety, dispute handling, financial records, service integrity, legal compliance, or operational accountability. Retained records will be limited to what is necessary for those purposes.

Contact

For account deletion questions, contact mindcarezw@gmail.com.

Emergency note: MindCare is not an emergency service. If someone is in immediate danger, they should contact local emergency services or a trusted nearby person immediately.
